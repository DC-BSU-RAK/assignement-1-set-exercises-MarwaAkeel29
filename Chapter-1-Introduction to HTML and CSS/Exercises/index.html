TIRAMISU

Tiramisu is a classic Italian dessert, including the robust taste of coffee-soaked ladyfingers, mascarpone cheese, and the subtle sweetness of whipped cream. The dessert is layered to perfection, offering a creamy texture and a delicate balance of sweetness from cream and bitterness from coffee. Whether you're celebrating a special occasion or indulging in a treat after a family meal, Tiramisu is sure to impress. With its elegant presentation and melt-in-your-mouth experience, it's perfect for sharing with friends and family. Jump to Recipe

Ingredients you'll need:

  • 6 large room temperature egg yolks
  • 3/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 cup of heavy cream
  • 1 cup of mascarpone cheese
  • 1 cup of your personal favorite strong brewed coffee or espresso (cold)
  • 24 ladyfinger biscuits (Savoiardi)
  • 1 tablespoon of Dutch-processed cocoa powder (for dusting)
  • 1 tsp of vanilla extract
  • Dark, milk, or white chocolate shavings (optional)
